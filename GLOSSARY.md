# Pattern Keepers

A short single-player puzzle game for MATH 10 (Mathematics in the Modern World). An apprentice weaver restores a lost loom pattern by solving mathematics puzzles in the rooms of a weaving house.

## World

**Apprentice**:
The player character, a young weaver in a fictional Philippine weaving village.
_Avoid_: Detective, hero, user

**Weaving House**:
The building where the whole game takes place, made up of three Rooms.
_Avoid_: Map, world, dungeon

**Loom**:
The instrument whose lost pattern the Apprentice must restore; restoring it is the goal of the game.
_Avoid_: Machine

## Structure

**Room**:
One of the three locked areas of the Weaving House, each holding three Puzzles on one theme; solving all three opens the door to the next Room.
_Avoid_: Level, stage, chapter (use "Level" only as a synonym when writing for the grader)

**Door Line**:
The single line of story shown when a Room's door opens.
_Avoid_: Cutscene, transition

**Puzzle**:
A single multiple-choice question with 3–4 Options, set in a Room.
_Avoid_: Question, challenge, quiz item

**Option**:
One of the answer choices shown for a Puzzle; exactly one is correct.
_Avoid_: Choice, answer button

**Distractor**:
An incorrect Option written to reflect a specific common mistake.
_Avoid_: Wrong answer, decoy

**Hint**:
A short nudge toward the correct Option, shown automatically after two wrong attempts on a Puzzle.
_Avoid_: Clue, tip

**Explanation**:
The screen shown after a Puzzle is solved, saying why the correct Option is right and why any Distractor the player chose was wrong.
_Avoid_: Solution screen, feedback, answer key
